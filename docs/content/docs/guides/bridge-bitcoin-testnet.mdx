## Deposit model: per-quote addresses, no memo

Bitcoin needs no memo, and 1Click itself gives Bitcoin quotes a plain deposit address. Tidepool does the same: every BTC-origin quote with `depositType: "ORIGIN_CHAIN"` gets its own native segwit `tb1q…` `depositAddress` (a key derived from the quote id and the server master seed). Send the exact `amountIn` in satoshis to it from any testnet4 wallet.

- `depositMode` is `SIMPLE` (the default). `ANY_INPUT` deposits are accepted, but an `ANY_INPUT` quote that pays out BTC is rejected.
- `POST /v0/deposit/submit` with `txHash` and `depositAddress` is optional: the relayer polls public Esplora APIs and detects the deposit anyway.
- After **1 confirmation** the relayer **sweeps** every confirmed output of the deposit address to the treasury in one transaction (the treasury absorbs the miner fee, so the credited amount equals what you sent), mints the NEP-141 on NEAR and credits it to the verifier. The sweep appears in the swap's `tidepool.explorer` list.
- Send the full amount in one transaction. A short deposit is refunded like on every other route.

## The BTC cap and minimum

Any swap with a BTC leg is rejected at **quote time** with a `400` if the BTC amount (input or output) is above `0.005 BTC` (500,000 sats) or below `0.0001 BTC` (10,000 sats, so miner fees stay small next to the amount). It covers swaps, payouts and refunds. Also:

- A payout of BTC is paid from the treasury, so quotes answer `503` if the treasury cannot cover the payout plus the miner fee.
- `ANY_INPUT` quotes that pay out BTC are rejected.
- Recipients and `refundTo` must be valid testnet addresses (`tb1…`, or legacy `m…`/`n…`/`2…`).

## 1. Get tBTC

**The Tidepool faucet never drips BTC**, to a wallet or to your intents balance (`POST /v0/faucet` with `chain: "btc"` answers `400` and names these links). Use a public testnet4 faucet:

- [CypherFaucet](https://cypherfaucet.com/btc-testnet): 0.01 tBTC, no sign-in.
- [testnet4.info faucet](https://testnet4.info) and other testnet4 faucets listed on [mempool.space/testnet4](https://mempool.space/testnet4).

Make sure the faucet is a **testnet4** faucet (testnet3 coins are on a different chain). The address format is the same.

## 2. BTC → NEAR

```ts
const quote = await OneClickService.getQuote({
  dry: false, swapType: 'EXACT_INPUT', slippageTolerance: 100,
  originAsset: 'nep141:btc.tp-omft.testnet',
  destinationAsset: 'nep141:usdc.tidepool-sandbox.testnet',
  amount: '100000',                          // 0.001 BTC in satoshis
  depositType: 'ORIGIN_CHAIN',
  refundTo: 'tb1qYourTestnet4Address', refundType: 'ORIGIN_CHAIN',
  recipient: 'you.testnet', recipientType: 'DESTINATION_CHAIN',
  deadline: new Date(Date.now() + 60 * 60_000).toISOString(),
});
const { depositAddress, amountIn } = quote.quote;
// Send amountIn satoshis to depositAddress on testnet4, then (optional):
await OneClickService.submitDepositTx({ txHash: '<btc txid>', depositAddress });
// Poll GET /v0/status?depositAddress=...
```

Give the quote a generous deadline: testnet4 blocks take about 10 minutes, but can be slower or faster. Runnable: `RECIPIENT=you.testnet npm run btc-deposit` in [tidepool-examples](https://github.com/zer0plugyut-afk/tidepool-examples) (it needs `BTC_PRIVATE_KEY_WIF`, a testnet4 key with a little tBTC, and prints the mempool.space links).

## 3. NEAR → BTC

Quote with `destinationAsset: 'nep141:btc.tp-omft.testnet'`, `recipient: 'tb1qYourAddress'` and `recipientType: 'DESTINATION_CHAIN'`, then deposit on NEAR as usual. The verifier `ft_withdraw`s the bridged token to the relayer, which pays out on testnet4 from the treasury; you receive exactly the quoted amount and the treasury pays the miner fee. Payouts are signed first and the signed transaction is recorded before it is broadcast, so a crash or retry re-broadcasts the same bytes and never pays twice.

## Refunds

Same reasons as every route (`DEADLINE_PASSED`, `PARTIAL_DEPOSIT`, …). A refund with `refundType: "ORIGIN_CHAIN"` is paid from the treasury to `refundTo` on testnet4 in the amount you sent.

## Limits

- **Testnet4 resets** are rare but possible; after one the balances are gone and the route has to be bootstrapped again.
- **Confirmation time.** A deposit is only credited after 1 confirmation, so a swap can wait on the next block. Payouts are broadcast as soon as the swap settles on NEAR and then need a block to confirm.
- **Fees.** The relayer pays miner fees from the treasury (at least 2 sat/vB, following the half-hour estimate of mempool.space). The fee is not deducted from your swap.
- **One treasury-signed transaction at a time.** Payouts share the treasury's coins and are serialized (unconfirmed change is tracked, so back-to-back payouts work).
- **Unreachable destinations.** If a payout keeps being rejected, the swap stays in `PROCESSING` with a note and needs operator attention.
- **Public APIs.** The relayer reads and broadcasts through the public mempool.space, blockstream.info and mempool.ninja testnet4 Esplora APIs (override: `BTC_API_URLS`), with failover, a shared read cache and exponential backoff on `429`. Public hosts rate-limit shared cloud IPs, so a deposit address is only polled every 20 seconds (every 2 minutes once its quote has expired) and rate limits can still delay a swap by a few minutes. `GET /v0/health` reports `bridges.chains.btc.ok: false` while the treasury cannot be read.

## Mapping to mainnet 1Click

Same request and response fields, `blockchain: "btc"`, a plain `depositAddress` and no memo. Mainnet's asset is `nep141:btc.omft.near` (8 decimals); on Tidepool it is `nep141:btc.tp-omft.testnet`. Take the asset id from `GET /v0/tokens` of the environment you are calling. Mainnet has no 0.005 BTC cap and uses real mainnet addresses (`bc1…`).
