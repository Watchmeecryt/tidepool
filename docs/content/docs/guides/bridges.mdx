## What matches 1Click (EVM chains)

- **Asset ids.** Bridged tokens look like 1Click's omft ids: `nep141:<chain>-<erc20 address>.tp-omft.testnet`. `/v0/tokens` lists them with `blockchain` and `contractAddress`.
- **Deposit addresses.** A live quote with an EVM `originAsset` and `depositType: "ORIGIN_CHAIN"` returns a unique `0x` deposit address. ERC-20: send with a plain `transfer` to a CREATE2 vault address. Native ETH: send value to a derived EOA (different address space than the vault).
- **Addresses.** `refundTo` and `recipient` must be `0x` addresses for EVM legs, and NEAR accounts for NEAR legs.
- **Status and refunds.** The same lifecycle and refund reasons. EVM transactions are listed in `originChainTxHashes` / `destinationChainTxHashes` with explorer links.

## How the EVM bridges work

The Solana, Stellar, Sui, Bitcoin and XRP Ledger bridges follow the same idea (relayer detects the deposit, mints the matching NEP-141 into the verifier, and pays out on the destination chain) but have their own deposit mechanics; see their route guides.

1. **Deposit.** Each quote's deposit address is a CREATE2 address of the chain's `TidepoolVault` (salt = `keccak256("tidepool-deposit:" + correlationId)`). Nothing is deployed there until funds arrive.
2. **Sweep.** The relayer calls `vault.sweep(salt, token)`. This deploys a tiny forwarder at the deposit address that sends the tokens to the vault and self-destructs in the same transaction.
3. **Credit.** The relayer mints the matching NEP-141 on NEAR and credits it into the verifier with a normal `ft_transfer_call`, to the quote's intents account. The swap settles with `execute_intents`, as on NEAR-only routes.
4. **Payout.** For an EVM destination (or an EVM refund), the verifier `ft_withdraw`s the bridged token to the relayer with memo `WITHDRAW_TO:0x…`. The relayer then calls `vault.release(id, token, to, amount)`. `id` is derived from the quote, and the vault refuses to release the same `id` twice.

## What you trust

- **The relayer key.** On the EVM chains, one key (`0x55F2c8044D790e1b8369508c31c756c0c2Ca0E93`) can sweep deposits and release any vault balance. If it is lost or misused, bridged balances are gone. There are no light clients, proofs or multisigs.
- **Minting.** Bridged NEP-141s are minted by the Tidepool root account. Supply on NEAR is not enforced against vault balances on-chain.
- **Liveness.** If the relayer is down, deposits wait in their deposit addresses and payouts wait as `PROCESSING`. Both resume when it restarts.
- **Testnet RPCs.** The relayer uses public RPCs. Congestion or reorgs can delay a swap.

Do not send mainnet assets to any of these addresses. Contracts on these chains do not accept real USDC or USDT; only the Tidepool test tokens listed in each route guide are credited.

## Gas and faucets

`POST /v0/faucet` with `{"chain":"base","address":"0x…"}` mints test USDC and USDT on that chain. It does not send gas: get testnet ETH from each chain's public faucet. The same call works for `sol`, `stellar` and `sui`, and `mode: "intents"` with `assets` chain aliases credits the bridged tokens to a NEAR intents balance. Pick a chain in the dropdown on the [Faucet](/docs/guides/faucet) page, or see its "How the faucet works" section.
