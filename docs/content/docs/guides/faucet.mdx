<Callout title="Faucet in the Tidepool App">
The [Tidepool App](https://app.usetidepool.tech/faucet) has two tabs: **Tokens** (one card per chain: NEAR, Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, BSC, Solana, Stellar, Sui and XRP, plus links for native BTC and gas) and **Stocks & RWAs** (pick up to 5 Ondo tokens on BSC Testnet). ## How the faucet works

The widget above and the API are the same faucet. In the UI you **pick a chain from the dropdown, paste your address on that chain, and press the button**: our backend signs and sends the tokens, you never sign anything. Through the API you send the same request yourself (examples below).

| Chain (`chain`) | Address you paste | What our backend sends | Where it lands | You still bring |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NEAR testnet (no `chain`) | `you.testnet` | Six NEAR test tokens | `wallet`: NEP-141 balance. `intents`: your balance inside the verifier | Nothing |
| Ethereum Sepolia (`eth`) | `0x…` | 100 test USDC + 100 test USDT (ERC-20) | Your address on Ethereum Sepolia | Sepolia ETH for gas |
| Base Sepolia (`base`) | `0x…` | 100 test USDC + 100 test USDT (ERC-20) | Your address on Base Sepolia | Base Sepolia ETH for gas |
| Arbitrum Sepolia (`arb`) | `0x…` | 100 test USDC + 100 test USDT (ERC-20) | Your address on Arbitrum Sepolia | Arbitrum Sepolia ETH for gas |
| BSC Testnet (`bsc`) | `0x…` | 100 test USDC + 100 test USDT (ERC-20 only; Stocks & RWAs are a separate tab) | Your address on BSC Testnet | BSC Testnet tBNB for gas |
| Stocks & RWAs (BSC) | `0x…` | Up to 5 Ondo tokens per claim (`assets: ["AAPLon",…]`); separate 7-day slot from Tokens | Your address on BSC Testnet | BSC Testnet tBNB for gas |
| Solana devnet (`sol`) | base58 wallet | 100 test USDC + 100 test USDT (SPL) | Your wallet's token accounts on Solana devnet (our backend creates them) | Devnet SOL for fees |
| Stellar testnet (`stellar`) | `G…` | 100 test USDC + 100 test USDT (classic assets) | Your account on Stellar testnet | An existing account with trustlines |
| Sui testnet (`sui`) | `0x…` (64 hex) | 100 test USDC + 100 test USDT (Move coins) | Your address on Sui testnet (any address, no setup) | Testnet SUI for gas |

- **Native gas is never dripped.** ETH, tBNB, SOL, XLM and SUI come from each chain's own public faucet ([Ethereum Sepolia](https://cloud.google.com/application/web3/faucet/ethereum/sepolia), [Base Sepolia](https://docs.base.org/get-started/get-funds), [Arbitrum Sepolia](https://faucet.quicknode.com/arbitrum/sepolia), [BSC Testnet](https://www.bnbchain.org/en/testnet-faucet), [Solana devnet](https://faucet.solana.com), [Stellar Friendbot](https://friendbot.stellar.org), [Sui testnet](https://faucet.sui.io)).
- **Stellar needs trustlines.** The account must exist (fund it with Friendbot) and add a `changeTrust` operation for each asset: `USDC` and `USDT`, both issued by `GDXABUSRHP4YA5CELHLNJQ4M3TX2BSJ6PEMDFCG2RA4PQLAQLRMFFQAN`. Without them the faucet answers `400` and names the missing one.
- **Native BTC is never dripped and has no entry in the dropdown.** `{"chain":"btc"}` and the `btc` intents alias answer `400` and name the external faucets for tBTC (testnet4): [CypherFaucet](https://cypherfaucet.com/btc-testnet) or [testnet4.info](https://testnet4.info). The treasury behind the [Bitcoin](/docs/guides/bridge-bitcoin-testnet) route is funded the same way, so it has a cap (0.005 BTC).
- **Native XRP is never dripped and has no entry in the dropdown.** `{"chain":"xrp"}` and the `xrp` intents alias answer `400` and point to the official XRPL testnet faucet, `POST https://faucet.altnet.rippletest.net/accounts` with `{"destination":"r…"}` (100 test XRP per request, no captcha; web version at [xrpl.org](https://xrpl.org/resources/dev-tools/xrp-faucets)). The treasury behind the [XRP Ledger](/docs/guides/bridge-xrp-testnet) route is funded the same way, so it has a cap (100 XRP). There is no USDC or USDT on the XRP Ledger route.
- **Sui needs nothing but an address.** The coins are minted to your address in one transaction; get SUI for gas from the Sui faucet.
- **Solana token accounts are created for you.** Our backend creates your associated token accounts for the two test mints and pays the rent, so a fresh wallet works. That costs treasury SOL, so there is a global daily cap.
- **Bridged tokens on NEAR.** The USDC and USDT on the other chains are also bridged into the NEAR verifier as `nep141:<chain>-….tp-omft.testnet` tokens. To get those into your **intents balance** (not to a wallet on that chain), use the NEAR chain, mode `intents`, and pick a bridged option in the UI, or pass chain aliases in `assets` (below).
- Test tokens have no value. They are minted by Tidepool's own testnet relayer, not by any NEAR or 1Click bridge.

### Pick the right door

| I want… | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Tokens to swap from a wallet on Base, Solana, Stellar, Sui… | That chain's entry in the dropdown (or `{"chain":…,"address":…}`) |
| Tokens ready to swap right now, inside the verifier | NEAR, mode `intents` (six NEAR tokens by default, or `assets` with chain aliases for bridged ones) |
| NEP-141 tokens in a NEAR wallet | NEAR, mode `wallet` |

### Call it from the API

```bash
# NEAR wallet (the default)
curl -X POST https://www.usetidepool.tech/v0/faucet -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"accountId":"alice.testnet","mode":"wallet"}'

# NEAR intents balance, default six tokens
curl -X POST https://www.usetidepool.tech/v0/faucet -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"accountId":"alice.testnet","mode":"intents"}'

# NEAR intents balance, bridged USDC + USDT of one chain (plus test SOL / XLM / SUI for sol / stellar / sui)
curl -X POST https://www.usetidepool.tech/v0/faucet -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"accountId":"alice.testnet","mode":"intents","assets":["base"]}'

# Ethereum Sepolia, Base Sepolia, Arbitrum Sepolia or BSC Testnet (eth, base, arb, bsc)
curl -X POST https://www.usetidepool.tech/v0/faucet -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"chain":"eth","address":"0xYourAddress"}'

# BSC Testnet Stocks & RWAs (Ondo; max 5 symbols; separate 7-day claim slot)
curl -X POST https://www.usetidepool.tech/v0/faucet -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"chain":"bsc","address":"0xYourAddress","assets":["AAPLon","TSLAon"]}'

# Solana devnet
curl -X POST https://www.usetidepool.tech/v0/faucet -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"chain":"sol","address":"YourSolanaAddress"}'

# Sui testnet
curl -X POST https://www.usetidepool.tech/v0/faucet -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"chain":"sui","address":"0xYourSuiAddress"}'

# Stellar testnet (account exists and trusts USDC and USDT)
curl -X POST https://www.usetidepool.tech/v0/faucet -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"chain":"stellar","address":"GYourStellarAddress"}'
```

### Responses

Response to `{"accountId":"alice.testnet","mode":"wallet"}`. This sample was recorded from a live drip and **shortened**: it shows two of the six NEAR test tokens in `drips` and only the first mint in `transactions`. A full response has one `drips` entry and (in `wallet` mode) one `transactions` entry for each of the six tokens; in `intents` mode, `transactions` entries are `{ assets, txHash, explorerUrl }`, one per six tokens:

```json
{
  "accountId": "alice.testnet",
  "mode": "wallet",
  "drips": [
    {
      "assetId": "nep141:usdc.tidepool-sandbox.testnet",
      "symbol": "USDC",
      "amount": "100"
    },
    {
      "assetId": "nep141:usdt.tidepool-sandbox.testnet",
      "symbol": "USDT",
      "amount": "100"
    }
  ],
  "transactions": [
    {
      "assetId": "nep141:usdc.tidepool-sandbox.testnet",
      "amount": "100",
      "txHash": "F6JiEXj2LSkzhAdGoBV5hBs5etTjdgNvvNsH72aBACcN",
      "explorerUrl": "https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/F6JiEXj2LSkzhAdGoBV5hBs5etTjdgNvvNsH72aBACcN"
    }
  ]
}
```

A rejected request returns `{ "message": "…" }` with HTTP 400 (for example `{"message":"accountId must be a NEAR testnet account id"}`) or 429 when rate limited (`Rate limited: … can use the faucet again after <time> (one claim per address every 7 days).`, plus `available_at` / `retryAfter`). Every field is in the [faucet reference](/docs/api-reference/tidepool-faucet).

### Bridged tokens into the verifier (`assets` chain aliases)

With `mode: "intents"`, `assets` accepts asset ids, NEAR symbols (`"USDC"` means the NEAR USDC) and these **chain aliases**: `sol`, `stellar`, `sui`, `base`, `arb`, `eth`, `bridged` (every chain at once). Each alias credits that chain's bridged test tokens to your intents balance. The default with no `assets` stays the six NEAR tokens.

| Alias | Tokens credited |
| --- | --- |
| `base`, `arb`, `eth` | 100 USDC + 100 USDT |
| `sol` | 100 USDC + 100 USDT + **0.05 test SOL** (`nep141:sol.tp-omft.testnet`) |
| `stellar` | 100 USDC + 100 USDT + **5 test XLM** (`nep141:stellar.tp-omft.testnet`) |
| `sui` | 100 USDC + 100 USDT + **0.005 test SUI** (`nep141:sui.tp-omft.testnet`) |
| `bridged` | All of the above |

The test SOL, XLM and SUI exist **only inside the verifier**: they are not native coins and are never sent to a wallet. Bridged drips share the same 7-day per-address claim limit as every other faucet variant. Bridged tokens cannot be requested in `wallet` mode (`400`).

| Token | Amount | Contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| USDC | 100 | `usdc.tidepool-sandbox.testnet` (6 decimals) |
| USDT | 100 | `usdt.tidepool-sandbox.testnet` (6 decimals) |
| wNEAR | 40 | `wnear.tidepool-sandbox.testnet` (24 decimals) |
| wBTC | 0.001 | `wbtc.tidepool-sandbox.testnet` (8 decimals) |
| ETH | 0.03 | `eth.tidepool-sandbox.testnet` (18 decimals) |
| XAUT | 0.03 | `xaut.tidepool-sandbox.testnet` (6 decimals) |

## NEAR modes

| `mode` | Where tokens land | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `wallet` | NEP-141 wallet balance. Minted fresh by the root account, one `mint` tx per token. | The account must exist on testnet. Storage is registered automatically. |
| `intents` | Your intents balance inside the verifier. One `mt_batch_transfer` from the maker. | Nothing. Works for any account id, even one that does not exist yet. |

Supply is not capped, but the faucet can only mint these fixed amounts. Only the token owner (`privacei.testnet`) and the two allowed minters (`tidepool-sandbox.testnet`, `maker.tidepool-sandbox.testnet`) can call `mint`.

See [POST /v0/faucet](/docs/api-reference/tidepool-faucet) for the full response.

## Limits

- **One claim per address every 7 days**, across every faucet variant (NEAR wallet, NEAR intents, bridged intents, and every other chain). Claiming on Base blocks the same `0x` on Ethereum Sepolia, and a NEAR `wallet` drip blocks `intents` for that account until the window ends.
- **Wallet age and IP cap:** NEAR accounts must exist and be at least 24 hours old, and EVM addresses must have sent a transaction or hold ETH on Ethereum Sepolia; otherwise you get `403` with a `reason`. Each IP can claim twice every 24 hours. If captcha is enabled, send `turnstileToken` (from the faucet page) or an `Authorization: Bearer naa_...` key.
- **Six drips per IP per hour**, across everything. Over the limit returns **HTTP 429** with `available_at` / `retryAfter` (and a `Retry-After` header). If a transfer fails, the limit is not consumed.
- Global daily caps protect the treasuries: Solana (it pays for token accounts), Stellar and Sui (it pays gas). Over the cap returns `429`.
- Do not loop the faucet; every drip is a fixed amount.

## Details per chain

- **EVM:** `{"chain":"base"|"arb"|"eth","address":"0x…"}` mints USDC and USDT (6 decimals). You can also call `faucet()` on the token contracts yourself. Addresses are in each route guide under [Test bridges](/docs/guides/bridges).
- **Solana devnet:** see the [Solana devnet bridge](/docs/guides/bridge-solana-devnet). SOL swaps are capped at 0.005 SOL.
- **Stellar testnet:** 7 decimals. See the [Stellar testnet bridge](/docs/guides/bridge-stellar-testnet). Get XLM from [Friendbot](https://friendbot.stellar.org) (10,000 test XLM).
- **Bitcoin testnet4:** native coins only, no faucet drip. See the [Bitcoin testnet4 bridge](/docs/guides/bridge-bitcoin-testnet).
- **XRP Ledger testnet:** native XRP only (6 decimals), no faucet drip. See the [XRP Ledger testnet bridge](/docs/guides/bridge-xrp-testnet).
- **Sui testnet:** USDC and USDT have 6 decimals. See the [Sui testnet bridge](/docs/guides/bridge-sui-testnet). SUI swaps are capped at 0.005 SUI. Get SUI from [faucet.sui.io](https://faucet.sui.io).

<Callout type="warn">Test tokens have **no value**. The bridged test tokens are bridged only by Tidepool's own testnet relayer, not by any NEAR or 1Click bridge. Do not list or trade them anywhere.</Callout>
