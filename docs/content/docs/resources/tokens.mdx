### Native ETH (delivery only, capped at 0.01 ETH)

| Token | Asset id | Decimals | Faucet drip |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ETH on Ethereum Sepolia | `nep141:eth.tp-omft.testnet` | 18 (capped at 0.01 ETH per swap) | none (bring gas from a [public Sepolia faucet](https://www.alchemy.com/faucets/ethereum-sepolia)) |
| ETH on Base Sepolia | `nep141:base.tp-omft.testnet` | 18 (capped at 0.01 ETH per swap) | none |
| ETH on Arbitrum Sepolia | `nep141:arb.tp-omft.testnet` | 18 (capped at 0.01 ETH per swap) | none |

These mirror 1Click's native-ETH ids `nep141:eth.omft.near`, `nep141:base.omft.near` and `nep141:arb.omft.near` (18 decimals, no contract address). Payouts are plain ETH transfers from the relayer wallet, so **any swap, payout or refund above 0.01 ETH is refused with a `400` at quote time**, `/v1` withdrawals have the same cap, and payout quotes answer `503` while the treasury is low (it keeps a gas reserve). Native ETH cannot be deposited to a chain address (`ORIGIN_CHAIN`), `ANY_INPUT` payouts are rejected, and the faucet never drips it. This is the gas you ask for after a swap to a fresh address: a few thousandths of ETH is enough. Maker inventory is small (0.05 ETH on Ethereum, 0.02 on Base and Arbitrum), so a large run may hit `Not enough maker liquidity`.

## Admin methods

| Method | Who | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `owner_id()` | view | Returns the owner. Currently `privacei.testnet` on all six. |
| `is_minter({ account_id })` | view | `true` for the owner and allowed minters. |
| `mint({ account_id, amount })` | owner or minter | Mints any amount. Used by the faucet. |
| `add_minter` / `remove_minter({ account_id })` | owner | Manage the minter allowlist. |
| `set_owner({ new_owner })` | owner | Transfers ownership and registers storage for the new owner. |

Current minters: `tidepool-sandbox.testnet` (wallet faucet) and `maker.tidepool-sandbox.testnet`.
